Location help & privacy

Allow location access when your browser asks. If blocked, enable location for this site in browser settings and check your phone’s Location Services, then try again.

Keep this page open and your screen awake while walking. Phones may pause updates when locked or when this page is in the background. The blue circle shows the phone’s reported uncertainty; it is not a guarantee of your exact position.

The dashed perimeter is an approximate OpenStreetMap boundary. Near it, or with poor GPS, your on-site status stays uncertain. Follow me requires two fresh, accurate fixes clearly inside. Off-site mode keeps the map at the Zoo. This is a browsing aid, not proof of attendance or enclosure identity.

Your location stays in this page’s memory and is cleared when you stop tracking. No location history is saved or sent to this app’s server. Leaflet loads from unpkg; map tiles load from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address and the areas of the map you view.
